Why the terminal is the fastest interface, and how I build tools that live in it.

Start with the claim: the terminal is the fastest interface you will ever use, and most GUI tools are slower for no benefit.

Why the terminal

Speed of intention — typing what you mean versus hunting for it.

Constraints that make tools good

  • Composability: text in, text out, exit codes that mean something.
  • Predictable flags, and sane defaults with no flags at all.
  • Fail loudly, and say what to do about it.

A tiny example

The smallest useful shape — one command, one job:

$ tool list --open
$ tool close 42 --note "fixed in #118"
